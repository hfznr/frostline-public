Privacy Policy

Frostline · Last updated 13 September 2026

Frostline collects no personal data. Nothing you do in the game is sent anywhere, because the game does not talk to any server at all.

What is collected

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash-reporting SDK and no third-party code of any kind. It makes no network requests, so no information — personal or otherwise — leaves your device.

What is stored on your device

Your best scores, total distance, collected crystals and your settings (sound, haptics, tilt steering) are saved locally on your iPhone using the system’s standard app storage. This data never leaves the device, is not readable by anyone else, and is deleted along with the app if you remove it.

Children

Because nothing is collected from anyone, nothing is collected from children either. The app has no accounts, no chat, no user-generated content, no social features and no links out to the web.

App Store data

Apple provides developers with anonymous, aggregated statistics about downloads and crashes through App Store Connect. That data comes from Apple, is not tied to individual people, and cannot be traced back to you by me. It is covered by Apple’s privacy policy, not this one.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever collects anything, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the App Store privacy labels will be updated with it.
