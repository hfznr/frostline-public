Reporting a problem

The more of this you can include, the faster it gets fixed:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • The app version (shown in Settings inside the game)
  • What you were doing when it happened — and whether it happens every time

Common questions

How do I play?
Swipe left and right to change lane, up to jump, down to slide. The main menu has a “How to Play” screen with the same thing in pictures.
Why does the bear catch me?
Every collision brings it one step closer, and the fourth one ends the run. Skiing cleanly for a while pushes it back again.
Are my scores backed up?
No. Scores are stored on the device only and are not sent anywhere, which also means deleting the app deletes them.
Does it need an internet connection?
No. The game works fully offline.
Is there an Android version?
Not currently. The game is built with Apple frameworks throughout.
